Research pillars

About DLER

Efficiency across the AI stack.

Led by Dr. Pavlo Molchanov, DLER is part of NVIDIA Research’s Learning and Perception Research group, led by Dr. Jan Kautz.

At the hardware layer, we reduce model memory, inference latency, and energy use. At the software layer, we develop efficient small models and reliable agentic systems.

Research

Four ways we make
AI work better.

  1. 01

    Efficient architectures

    Novel model designs that improve the capability-to-compute ratio, from hybrid language models to flexible networks.

    Models
  2. 02

    Post-training optimization

    Compression, sparsity, quantization, distillation, and neural architecture search for practical deployment.

    Systems
  3. 03

    Universal representations

    Powerful and efficient representations of images, video, language, and the connections between them.

    Multimodal
  4. 04

    Reliable agentic systems

    Efficient, dependable language and multimodal model applications for assistants, robotics, and autonomous driving.

    Agents
